# Lesson 1 Plan - A Private Conversation

This extension-only planning card supports backfilling the extension practice
for Lesson 1. The main courseware was produced before per-lesson cards existed.

## Basic Info

- Lesson: 1
- Title: `A private conversation`
- Chinese title: `私人谈话`
- Core bridge: `公共空间里的私人边界。`

## Extension Practice Direction

Lesson 1 extension production should be extension-only. Create
`web/data/extension/lesson_1.json` and register `1` in
`web/data/extension/index.json`. Do not regenerate script, audio, timeline,
read-aloud data, living-scene data, or storyboard images.

### Extension Reading

Use an original modern public-space story about someone talking loudly during a
film, lecture, online class, or library study session. Do not retell the theatre
plot. Keep the social lesson clear: a conversation can be private in content
but still disturb other people if it is loud in a shared place.

Suggested title: `Too Loud in the Library`

Recommended word bank: `private`, `conversation`, `seat`, `loudly`, `pay
attention`, `angry`, `rudely`, `shared space`, `polite reminder`, `none of your
business`.

The 5 reading questions should test:

1. where the shared-space problem happened
2. what made the conversation disturb others
3. how the narrator first reacted
4. why the rude answer was not acceptable
5. the social lesson: private content still needs public manners

### Sentence Writing

Use 4 patterns:

1. `I had a very good ...`
   - Source: `I had a very good seat.`
   - Focus: setting up an expectation before a problem appears.
   - Must include: `had a very good`
2. `did not pay any attention`
   - Source: `They did not pay any attention.`
   - Focus: ignoring someone else's signal or request.
   - Must include: `did not pay any attention`
3. `In the end, I could not ...`
   - Source: `In the end, I could not bear it.`
   - Focus: reaching the limit after repeated annoyance.
   - Must include: `In the end` and `could not`
4. `It's none of your business`
   - Source: `It's none of your business.`
   - Focus: a rude boundary sentence; understand it, but use carefully.
   - Must include: `none of your business`
